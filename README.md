**AuthorPlay** is a simple and accessible streaming platform designed to help small and emerging artists get heard. With AuthorPlay, independent creators can share their work, reach new audiences, and build their presence in the creative world.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Easy Uploads**: Artists can quickly upload their content and make it available for streaming.
- **Inclusive Platform**: Focused on supporting small and independent creators.
- **Streaming Made Simple**: A user-friendly interface for both artists and audiences.
